How to Delete Your JagSpoor Account

Last updated: 10 Oct 2026

If you use JagSpoor (za.co.jagspoor.app), you can delete your account and associated data at any time.

Option 1: Delete directly in the app (recommended)

  1. Open JagSpoor app
  2. Go to Settings > Hunter Profile > Delete Account
  3. Confirm deletion
  4. Your Firebase Auth user, Hunter Profile (name/surname/phone/email), tracks, spoor photos, and Firestore data are permanently deleted within 7 days. Some anonymized logs may be retained for 30 days for security.

Option 2: Request deletion via email

If you cannot access the app, email privacy@jag-spoor.co.za from your registered email with subject "Delete My Account". Include your registered phone number. We will delete within 7 business days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Auth account, profile, tracks (lat/lng), spoor images, Firestore documents, Storage files
  • What is retained: anonymized security logs max 30 days, as required by POPIA
