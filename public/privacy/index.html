Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

JAGSPOOR VELD AND VENTURES ("JagSpoor", "we", "us") is the responsible party (data controller) for the personal information processed through the JagSpoor hunting ecosystem application, as defined by the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA"). This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, how we store and share it, and the rights you have over it.

POPIA registration number: 2026-066026 · Company registration (CIPC): 2026/675772/07 · Information Officer: Donald Llewelyn Kearney · Privacy contact: privacy@jag-spoor.co.za

2. Information we collect

  • Account and contact information — your first name, surname, phone number and email address, captured from the mandatory Hunter Profile, together with your account role and an optional profile photo.
  • Location information — device GPS latitude/longitude collected while you create a track, log a waypoint or record a harvest location. Location is used only while the relevant feature is active and is cached on your device so off-grid navigation works without signal.
  • Photos of spoor and game — camera or gallery images you attach to spoor analyses, trophies and bug reports.
  • Device and diagnostic information — app version, platform, push-notification (FCM) tokens and a one-way hashed device fingerprint used to prevent free-trial abuse.

3. Purpose of processing

  • Offline spoor validation — the spoor identifier computes contour, circularity, aspect ratio and Otsu-threshold measurements on your device to validate a track. The image analysis runs locally on the phone.
  • Hunter Mode profiles — your Hunter Profile powers your account and your hunting records.
  • Dual-stamp tracking — hunt tracks and location records are stamped with an owner identifier so your data stays scoped to you.
  • Referral sharing — referral codes are shared through App Links (https://jag-spoor.co.za/r/<CODE>) and a custom app scheme.
  • Bookings, permits, trophies and support — to operate marketplace bookings, venison transport permits, the trophy room and the in-app support channel, and to deliver booking-status notifications.

4. Legal basis (POPIA)

POPIA section 11 requires a lawful basis for every processing activity. We rely on:

  • Performance of a contract — to create your account and deliver the app features you request.
  • Compliance with a legal obligation — firearm-licence records, hunter/permit verification and animal-transport permit particulars required by South African law.
  • Your explicit consent — location tracking, photos, push notifications and any health or emergency information you choose to provide (special personal information under POPIA section 26 and 27).
  • Legitimate interest — securing the platform, preventing free-trial abuse and de-identified product analytics.

We never sell your personal information and we do not share your health, ID or firearm details with other app users.

5. Storage and security

Personal information is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage). Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+), encrypted at rest by Google Cloud, and protected by owner-scoped Firestore security rules. Firebase infrastructure may store data on servers located in the United States and the European Union. POPIA section 72 permits this cross-border transfer because Google is contractually bound to a level of protection substantially similar to POPIA.

7. Your rights

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you (POPIA s.23).
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate or out-of-date information (POPIA s.24).
  • Deletion — request erasure of your personal information (POPIA s.24). You can also delete your account yourself in the app (see below).
  • Objection to marketing — object to direct marketing at any time (POPIA s.69).
  • Complaint — complain to our Information Officer and, if unresolved, to the Information Regulator (POPIA s.74).

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@jag-spoor.co.za.

Deleting your account in the app

  1. Open JagSpoor and go to Hunter Profile (Settings → Hunter Profile).
  2. Scroll to the PRIVACY & DATA section.
  3. Tap DELETE MY ACCOUNT & DATA and confirm.

Outfitter accounts use Outfitter Settings → DANGER ZONE → DELETE ACCOUNT & ALL DATA. Your account and associated personal information are permanently removed within 30 days.

8. Children

JagSpoor is a hunting application intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly process the personal information of a child under 18 without the consent of a competent person (POPIA s.35). If you believe a child's information has been submitted, contact privacy@jag-spoor.co.za and we will erase it.
