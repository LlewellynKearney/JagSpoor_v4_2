Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 Oct 2026

1. Introduction

JagSpoor (za.co.jagspoor.app) is the ultimate South African hunting companion. This policy explains how we handle your data under POPIA.

2. Information we collect

  • Hunter Profile: First Name, Surname, Phone, Email (mandatory)
  • Location: Latitude/Longitude when you create a track
  • Photos: Spoor images for offline validation (contour/circularity/aspect/Otsu)
  • Device: Diagnostics via Firebase

3. Purpose

Offline spoor validation, Hunter Mode profiles, dual-stamp tracking, referral sharing via App Links https://jag-spoor.co.za/r/

4. Legal Basis

POPIA - legitimate interest and consent for hunting log service.

5. Storage

Firebase Firestore/Storage, encrypted in transit (TLS), hosted US/EU. We do not sell data.

6. Retention

Until you delete your account. Tracks remain until deleted in-app.

7. Your Rights

Access, correction, deletion via privacy@jag-spoor.co.za or in-app Settings > Delete Account.

8. Children

App is 18+ hunting related, not directed to children.
